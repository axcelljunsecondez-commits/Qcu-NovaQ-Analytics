## Derivations used by the repairs

M2: stationarity and identical exponential service make the aggregate occupancy chain independent of the work-conserving priority order. Normalize its birth/death weights to obtain P(all busy). Conditional on all c servers busy, the first completion has mean1/(c mu). This yields W0. MIT's class formula then uses cumulative class utilizations in the two denominators. Consequently lambda1 Wq1 + lambda2 Wq2 must equal the aggregate Lq. This conservation identity is an additional oracle, not the only validation of class waits. The book's equation images were read directly; the current code's numerator and Vol.I§3.3 attribution both conflict with that evidence.

M3, independent quantity-times-rate derivation:

- Counter-hours cH times currency/counter-hour gives cH Cs.
- Expected customers lambda H times modeled hours/customer Wq gives customer-hours; times currency/customer-hour gives lambda H Wq Cw.
- Expected abandonments lambda H p times currency/abandonment gives lambda H p Ca.

Thus total = H(c Cs + lambda Wq Cw + lambda p Ca). The synthetic reference c2, lambda4/h, Wq.25h, Cs10, Cw100, p.1, Ca5 gives 122H. This is a modeled steady-interval accounting quantity, not measured employee pay or an abandoned customer's observed wait. The newer shared-day event-based costing and optimizer hourly objective remain unchanged. Supported interpretation assumes finite nonnegative duration; this repair adds no new duration validation or input-policy changes. Existing scalar/vector rounding paths remain unchanged.
